Agriculture Farm

Self-Sustainable Model Farm for Teaching and Research Excellence and Profit Maximization

The Agriculture Farm plays an important role in the School of Agriculture's ambition of becoming a premier agricultural school providing high-class agricultural education. Supporting the PNG University of Technology's drive to grow world-class technocrats for the real world, it serves as an agricultural field lab for teaching, research and demonstration work by students and academics — combining livestock, crops, machinery and a process plant into a single benchmark of a self-sustainable model farm.

1. Teaching & Research Excellence

The Agriculture Farm serves as a training ground for old and new agricultural techniques through practical sessions for undergraduate and vocational-technical students working through the BSAG 1–4 programme.

Practical sessions and research trials on agricultural techniques by students studying towards their Masters and Doctorate degrees are also conducted at the farm, giving postgraduates a working field lab for genuine experimentation rather than a purely theoretical setting.

2. Livestock

The livestock section includes beef cattle, piggery, ruminants (sheep and goat), and poultry — layers, broilers and ducks. The combined commercial activities of these operations help drive the farm's mission of profit maximization.

Duck and fish, one system. A planned integration of poultry (duck) and aquaculture (fish) production is the next step in expanding the livestock section.

3. Crops

Rice, taro, sweet potato, cassava, aibika, coconuts (dwarf and tall) and cocoa are currently grown at the farm. Bud grafting — a vegetative propagation technique — is an ongoing operation carried out at the cocoa nursery to raise cocoa clone seedlings.

  • Staple crops — rice, taro, sweet potato, cassava and aibika grown for teaching, research and sale.
  • Perennial & tree crops — dwarf and tall coconut varieties alongside cocoa, propagated through the nursery's bud-grafting programme.

4. Machineries

The machinery and engineering section includes the tractor and its implements, used for slashing, ploughing, harrowing and transportation. This section supports teaching and research, livestock, and crop production alike — very little else on the farm moves without it.

5. Process Plant

A newly established and refurbished slaughter house is in use to process whole (meat) birds for supply to the PNGUoT Mess, the wider PNGUoT community, and the general public.

6. Biosecurity & Quarantine

The Agriculture Farm implements a high standard of biosecurity to control the introduction of pests and disease from outside, and to minimise the development of pests and disease within the farm. Stationary policies and regulations are adhered to effectively across every section.

Why this matters

Livestock, crops and people move through the same farm every day. Without disciplined biosecurity and quarantine practice, a single lapse can undo years of breeding, planting and research work — which is why protocols are enforced consistently rather than only when an outbreak is suspected.
