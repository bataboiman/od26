PNG Analytica

Empowering research at PNG University of Technology through quality chemical analysis, testing and advisory services for stakeholders.

PNG Analytica is the analytical laboratory serving the Papua New Guinea University of Technology and its partners. Formerly the University Analytical Services Laboratory (UASL), it was established under a strategic partnership between PNGUoT and the Australian Centre for International Agricultural Research (ACIAR) to advance analytical standards in the region — supporting teaching, research, student training and stakeholder advisory services.

1. From UASL to PNG Analytica

Established under a strategic partnership between PNG University of Technology and ACIAR, the laboratory was created to advance analytical standards in the region. It exists to provide analytical and advisory services for teaching, research and student training across the university and its partner stakeholders.

A regional asset built on partnership. From its UASL roots to the present, the laboratory brings internationally aligned analytical practice to research, teaching and advisory work across PNG.

2. Vision, Goals & Core Values

PNG Analytica’s vision is to be the premier analytical laboratory providing quality services at budget costs. Its goals are to maintain an ISO 17025:2017 and ISO 9001:2015 compliant quality system, and to operate on an entrepreneurial footing so it remains sustainable.

  • Integrity — every result is honest and defensible.
  • Quality — an ISO-aligned system governing every step.
  • Honesty — clear reporting to every stakeholder.
  • Safety — for staff, samples and the environment.
  • Good Science — methods that stand up to scrutiny.

3. Key Laboratory Activities

The laboratory carries out five core activities for university researchers and external stakeholders:

  • Diagnostic testing — of biological tissue samples for nutritional disorders.
  • Determination — of soil fertility status for plant-available nutrients.
  • Analysis — of food and animal feed for nutritional element content.
  • Analysis — of sediments for basic element concentration.
  • Diagnostics — of toxicity in degraded soils (mining and industrial).

4. MIR High-Throughput Diagnostic Capability

PNG Analytica applies mid-infrared (MIR) spectroscopy as a high-throughput diagnostic capability. A single rapid scan produces a spectral “fingerprint” from which validated models predict a range of crucial soil and physical parameters — without the many slow, reagent-heavy steps of traditional wet-testing.

Predictable parameters include soil fertility profiles (organic carbon, total nitrogen, EC, available phosphorus, cation-exchange capacity) and physical properties (soil texture — sand, silt, clay — and moisture), calibrated against locally developed PNG datasets.

One scan, many answers — at low overhead. MIR turns a spectrum into validated soil predictions for roughly K49 per scan, with minimal reagent overhead and fast turnaround.

5. Quality System & Economics

Quality is governed by an aligned ISO/IEC 17025:2017 (testing and calibration) and ISO 9001:2015 (management) system. MIR predictive models are validated against locally developed PNG datasets so results are defensible for research, teaching and advisory work.

Because a single rapid scan replaces many reagent-heavy wet-tests, the laboratory keeps unit economics low — on the order of K49 per scan — supporting an entrepreneurial, sustainable operating model that keeps services affordable for university and stakeholder clients.

Why standards matter

Analytical results only mean something if the system behind them is defensible. Without ISO-aligned processes and locally validated models, the same scan can quietly produce different “truth” for different clients — which is why the quality system is not an add-on but the foundation of every result PNG Analytica issues.

6. Who We Serve

The laboratory serves the whole university — providing analytical and advisory services for teaching and research across the sciences, and hands-on student training in modern analytical methods. It is equally available to external stakeholders who need defensible analytical data and advisory support.

Clients include university schools and centres, postgraduate and undergraduate researchers, and external partners requiring soil, tissue, food, feed or sediment analysis — anywhere an independently validated result carries weight.

7. Analytical Process Flow

Every sample moves through the same aligned pipeline. After intake and preparation, the appropriate route is chosen — a rapid MIR scan where a validated model exists, or traditional wet-testing where it does not. Results are validated against the quality system before a report reaches the stakeholder.

  1. Intake & preparation — samples received, logged and prepared for analysis.
  2. Analysis — MIR rapid scan or wet-testing, matched to the parameter.
  3. Validation — ISO 17025:2017 / 9001:2015 aligned checks before release.
  4. Report — a defensible, traceable result delivered to the stakeholder.
